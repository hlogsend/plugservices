About PlugServices

La culture, célébrée. — Celebrating African culture across Canada & USA.

Who we are

PlugServices is an event promotion platform managed by Plug Trading Services Inc. — where the African diaspora in Canada and USA finds what's happening and buys tickets in seconds. From Afrobeats nights and comedy tours to cultural festivals, business conferences, national-day celebrations and galas, we bring the culture together in one place. Listing is free — 0% per ticket (bank processing fees may apply).

We started as an event-promotion company celebrating multicultural talent. Today we're focused on what our community actually shows up for: African diaspora events of every kind — nightlife, family, cultural and professional — promoted bilingually in English and French and sold through secure online ticketing.

What we do

  • For guests: discover events by city and vibe, and buy secure QR tickets in seconds.
  • For organizers: list events free, reach the African diaspora audience, and get fast payouts with promotion, email marketing, sound & stage, and on-site support.
  • For artists: get linked to festivals, activities and stages that celebrate diversity and creativity.
